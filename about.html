Jodie Luk Lai Wa
Work COMMISSION About

Jodie Luk Lai Wa (b.1996, Hong Kong), based in Hamburg (Germany) and Hong Kong, is an artist and filmmaker working with moving image and installation. She obtained MFA Public Art and New Artistic Strategies at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar and BFA Film and Television at the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts.
